We also preconditioned $Q_2$ with smoothed-aggregation algebraic multigrid (pyamg) using rigid-body near-nullspace modes. With the default pyamg settings, the number of CG iterations grew from 16 to 1700 as refinement stretched the cells, and CG did not converge within 2000 iterations from 193\,980 unknowns onwards; with an evolution strength measure designed for anisotropy, the number of CG iterations grew from 12 to 1589 as refinement stretched the cells, and CG did not converge within 2000 iterations from 283\,324 unknowns onwards. On the same grids, the fast-diagonalization preconditioner needed 11 to 20 CG iterations. These AMG settings were not tuned for the extreme anisotropy of adaptively refined tensor grids, and better-suited AMG variants may close the gap; the complete data are in \url{results/E13.json}.
